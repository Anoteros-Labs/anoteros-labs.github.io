Kickback Support

Kickback is a browser extension that shows you which of your friends are watching on Twitch, and lets you join them. It is currently in a small private beta.

Get in touch

Email us about anything to do with Kickback:

  • help using it, or something that is not working
  • bug reports
  • questions about how it works
  • privacy questions and data requests
  • deleting your account and the data attached to it

anoteros.dev@gmail.com

Feedback from inside Kickback

You can also send feedback without leaving Twitch. Open the Kickback panel, click your avatar in the top right to open the account panel, and choose Feedback. Pick a category — Bug, Confusing, Idea or Other — write what happened, and send it.

Feedback sent that way reaches us with a small amount of technical context about your Kickback session, which usually saves a round of questions. What that context contains is listed in full in the Privacy Policy.
